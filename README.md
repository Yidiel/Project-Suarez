# Project Suarez

The strategy home for UFO Suarez, the brand and design studio of Yidiel Suarez. It is the shared playbook for Yid and his agents.

- `strategy/01-audit.md`: the current state vs. Oren John's personal brand, creative direction and brand business principles.
- `strategy/02-game-plan.md`: the one-line offer, the price ladder, the Stage 1 channel, Place Studies and the 90-day plan (v2).
- `agents/ROSTER.md`: the six agent roles, handoffs and templates.
- `transcripts/notes/`: timestamped notes on Oren's 16 priority videos. These are the primary source for v2.
- `research/oren-john-notes.md`: v1 notes from his newsletter, kept for background.
- `transcripts/`: where real video transcripts go.
