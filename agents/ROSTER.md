## Creative Director: brief template

Based on Oren's Creative Direction 101 and the reference methods.

1. **The idea in one line,** plus the strategy it serves: what is true about this brand.
2. **Archetype and tone.**
3. **Three anchor references,** each remixed through scale, aesthetic, transportation, tension and opposites. Keep the strongest idea from each.
4. **Board** of 10 or more images: compositions, type, color, texture, photography direction.
5. **Throughline:** the one visual thread every touchpoint must carry, and where the brand goes the other way from its category.
6. **Applications list,** with the throughline shown on each.
7. **What we won't do.**

## Archivist: case study template

The brand website as a library: teach how the work was done, not just show it.

1. The situation, the constraint, and the deadline.
2. The brief, in one line.
3. References and board, the before.
4. The system: mark, type, color, rules.
5. Applications in the real world, using your photography.
6. The result: launch and client words.
7. A 5-beat cutdown for Showrunner Pillar 1.
