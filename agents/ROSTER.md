# UFO Suarez agent roster

Six roles. **Existing** means a skill or role you already use; **New** means it should be built (with skill-creator) once you approve the brief. Every agent reads `strategy/02-game-plan.md` and the Point of View page before working.

House writing style, carried over from your existing skills: casual and plain, no em dashes, decision-first. Give options with a one-line why, so Yid picks rather than composes.

## Creative Director: brief template

From Creative Director 101 [xqfrHgRGCCA 1:15, 11:50] and Mastering Moodboards [GSBjVedREWE 6:45].

**Brief 101 fields** (Oren's list):
- Title
- Creative type
- Purpose and ask
- Number of assets
- Destination
- Ratios
- Due date
- Links to content
- References
- Notes

**UFO Suarez additions:**
1. **The idea in one line,** plus the strategy it serves: what is true about this place.
2. **Client stage:** which of his 7 stages the client is in, and what work that stage needs [UBDpGwdkiz4].
3. **Reference remix:** run 3 anchor references through scale, aesthetic, beauty, transportation, clashing and tension. Keep the strongest idea from each.
4. **Boards:** one vibe board, then tactical boards (type, color, photo, logo) for sign-off one decision at a time.
5. **The one thing** for each key image, plus the composition, action, location and light notes [Ku9bcxICrJQ].
6. **Throughline,** plus hidden "Easter egg" details tied to the place's history [z08CfQZE6TI 7:06].
7. **What we won't do.**

## Archivist: case study template

The brand website as a library: teach how the work was done, not just show it.

1. The situation, the constraint, and the deadline.
2. The brief, in one line.
3. References and board, the before.
4. The system: mark, type, color, rules.
5. Applications in the real world, using your photography.
6. The result: launch and client words.
7. A 5-beat cutdown the Showrunner can post.

## Showrunner: Place Study package
- The subject (from the Top 30), and why them.
- The one thing that makes their identity work, then "here's what I'd do."
- The hook or title and cover image, done first.
- The 5-beat script or carousel copy.
- A CTA tied to the funnel stage: top of funnel none, middle "see the full case study", bottom "taking 2 launch projects for Q1".
