# VSL merch deck: Round 1 outline (60 slides)

Audience: Yid (Round 1 review). Round 2 goes to the VSL team. Format: HTML deck, 16:9, exportable to PDF.
Design rules: no eyebrows (no small tracked-caps label lines above headlines, no coordinate or elevation strings as decoration, no label-value UI blocks). No Park City. No Ski Utah, no Olympic marks, no state tourism slogan. Plain sentences.

## Part 0. Open (1 to 4)
1. Cover: title of the line, Visit Salt Lake, Round 1, date.
2. The problem: what VSL sells today is basic logo merch.
3. The airport test: if you can buy it at the airport or a local shop, it isn't it.
4. What this deck does: context, foundation, executions, build. How to read it.
