# Latino lane, concept round 1

Lane thesis (CD-approved): Latin life in the West, no invented taglines, words from the community. Fútbol and lowrider culture, hand lettering, no celebrity. Cut from the board: the gothic rocker, Bad Bunny, the quinceañera.

**Who designs this.** These three concepts are a starting brief, not finished artwork. The line must be designed by Latino designers from Salt Lake County (West Valley, Rose Park, Glendale first), with a letterer and an illustrator from the community redrawing everything here, and a revenue share written into the deal with a community org (Centro Civico Mexicano or Comunidades Unidas; the Kids of Immigrants x CHIRLA model, org on the hangtag, money going back). VSL's role is licensor and curator, not author. The test for every piece: would the community wear it to a Sunday car show.

Shared label on all three: interior neck label with the small salt-crystal line icon, "Diseñado en Salt Lake County," and the partner-org line. No VSL logo in the artwork.

Fonts in the SVGs are stand-ins named by Google font (Pirata One, Anton, Pinyon Script, Archivo Narrow). If the deck machine lacks them, the files fall back to system faces; the production art is hand-drawn anyway.

## 01 · Club Deportivo Salt Lake jersey
File: `01-club-deportivo-salt-lake-jersey.svg`

**IN references:** 09-paisaboys-club-deportivo-tee.jpg, 10-paisaboys-doce-estrellas-jacket.jpg, 03-bxr-worldwide-mexico.jpg, 07-koi-nike-jersey.jpg, 35-sltrib-la-barra-real-2021.jpg, 42-ksl-latino-world-cup-utah-2026.jpg.

**Idea.** A club jersey for a club that is the city: crest (brine shrimp curled over the Wasatch ridge, CLUB DEPORTIVO / SALT LAKE · 1847, 801 at the foot), eight sleeve stars for the eight places the community lives, SALT LAKE arched in blackletter and 801 in block on the back. It borrows the Paisa Boys crest-and-year logic and the Born x Raised flag-color jersey back, and points them at Salt Lake instead of Guadalajara.

**Garment and fabric.** A jersey is the most-worn garment in this community (KOI x Nike proved it sells as fashion). 160 gsm recycled polyester bird's-eye mesh, custom-cut, drop shoulder, v-neck with Forest rib collar and cuffs, sized up and worn loose over a tee the way it is worn at the watch party.

**Technique.** Crest as a 3.5 in embroidered patch (a patch is what a club does; it can also be sold alone). Stars as 1 in stitched twill appliqués, not prints, so they read as the Doce Estrellas jacket does. Back name and number in 2-color tackle twill on the hero tier, sublimated on the retail tier. Hem names screen-printed so the star count is explained on the garment.

**Colorway.** Salt Pink body, Forest trim and ink, Cream keyline. Pink is a real fútbol color (Palermo, Inter Miami, Mexico's 2024 away) and it is the lake, so it is ours. Claret and cobalt is listed as an alt for RSL match days only, with the club's blessing; without RSL behind it, it reads as a knockoff kit.

**Decisions to verify.** The eight stars: West Valley, Rose Park, Glendale, Magna, Kearns, Midvale, South Salt Lake, Taylorsville. Two of those are Salt Lake City neighborhoods and six are separate municipalities or metro townships in the county, so "eight" is a community count, not a census category. The community designers should set the final list and count (a Salt Lake City-only version would be six: Rose Park, Glendale, Poplar Grove, Fairpark, Jordan Meadows, Westpointe). 1847 is the city founding year; 801 is the 1947 area code (check that no local brand owns an 801 mark). The crest's brine shrimp should be redrawn scientifically, segmented and feathery, not cute.

**What a local would say.** "Which club is that?" "Nosotros."

**Risk.** It looks like a supporters' kit for a club that does not exist, and RSL and La Barra Real could read it as competition rather than tribute. Bring La Barra Real into the room before the first sample; a co-signed version for the barra is the upside of the same risk.

## 02 · Vecino
File: `02-vecino-fine-line-tee.svg`

**IN references:** 01-bxr-hyna-tee.jpg, 25-mister-cartoon-by-oriol-1994.jpg, 05-koi-this-is-for-our-family-tee.jpg, 13-estevan-oriol-lamag.jpg, 26-cdmx-rotulos-zocalo.jpg.

**Idea.** One word, set huge across the chest in a single-weight fine-line script, no explanation, the Born x Raised HYNA move with the Mister Cartoon hand. The word is Vecino, neighbor: what the West Side calls itself, what Comunidades Unidas and Centro Civico say in their own copy, and a plain statement a visitor can wear honestly only if they act like one.

**Why Vecino and not the others.** Paisa is Paisa Boys' brand name and we are already borrowing their logic. Raza carries political weight VSL has no standing to use. Hyna is Born x Raised's. Familia and Barrio are on a thousand tees already. Oeste is the right place but the community says "West Side" in English. Vecino belongs equally to Mexicans, Venezuelans, Salvadorans and Peruvians, nobody owns it, and it means something in the mouth of a tourism office without being a tagline. The women's cut carries VECINA, same drawing, one letter changed by the same hand.

**Garment and fabric.** 7.5 oz ringspun cotton, boxy, drop shoulder, garment-dyed black and enzyme-washed so the surface is soft enough for a hairline. The BxR tee is black with white ink for a reason: the line needs the contrast.

**Technique.** One-color white water-based screen print, 13 x 5 in, 230 mesh, one pass. A letterer redraws it: Pinyon Script is a placeholder for a hand like Cartoon's, single-weight 0.5 pt line, script capitals, the tail of the last letter becoming the underline. Ink on vellum, scanned, vectorized. Back neck: 801 in the same hand at 1.25 in. Hem: "Diseñado en Salt Lake County," plain.

**What a local would say.** "That's it? Yeah, that's it."

**Risk.** Fine-line script is unforgiving in production: if the mesh or the ink is wrong the line fills and the tee looks cheap. Strike-offs on the actual blank before any run, and keep the letterer in the approval chain.

## 03 · Lotería Salt Lake
File: `03-loteria-salt-lake-tee.svg`

**IN references:** 47-loteria-boards.jpg, 26-cdmx-rotulos-zocalo.jpg, 15-ksl-hispanic-heritage-utah.jpg, 19-sltrib-lowrider-urban-arts-2019.jpg, 20-ksl-utah-lowrider-community.jpg, 41-deseret-jaripeo-days-of-47-2019.jpg.

**Idea.** A six-card Salt Lake lotería board on the back of a heavyweight black tee, one card (El Camarón) on the left chest: El Trax, La Montaña, El Taco, La Sal, El Camarón, La Troca, drawn flat in Don Clemente proportions with a numeral top-left and the name on the foot rule. The numbers are local facts, not 1 to 6: 02 (TRAX opened for 2002), 11 (Wasatch elevation in thousands of feet), 03 (three al pastor on a plate), 26 (north-arm salinity), 21 (DWR's 21 cysts per liter), 47 (1847 and Days of '47, the jaripeo weekend).

**Garment and fabric.** 7.5 oz ringspun cotton, boxy, side-seamed, garment-dyed Ink black, XS to 4XL because car-club sizing runs big. Black makes the lotería palette pop the way the papel picado backdrop does on the Viva La Bonita shot.

**Technique.** 9-color spot screen print on a Cream underbase, 11 x 11 in board on the back, 3.4 x 5.1 in single card on the front chest. Spot color, no halftones: lotería cards are flat ink and the print should feel like a card stock, not a photo. Card palette is all brand hex (Light Sky, Salt Pink, Sego Lily, Sage, Aspen Gold, Meadow, Rodeo Red, Terracotta, Mountain Navy, Cream). A Salt Lake illustrator redraws all six; the SVG drawings are blocking. Extension: the six become a real card set sold in the paper line, and three more cards (El Lowrider, La Tejana, La Arepa for the Venezuelan community) make the second drop.

**Colorway.** Ink body, full card palette. No off-palette hit needed.

**What a local would say.** "Where's El Lowrider?" (Second drop. Tell them.)

**Risk.** Lotería is the most-borrowed Mexican visual there is, and "Lotería [City]" tees exist for a dozen cities; done by the wrong hands this is airport merch. The thing that saves it is the numbers, the specific objects (the TRAX car, the brine shrimp, the salt cube) and a community illustrator's hand. If the illustrator is not from here, cut it.
