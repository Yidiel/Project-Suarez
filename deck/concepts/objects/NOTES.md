# Lane 3, Objects. Round 1 notes

Lane thesis (approved): the unseen objects of a Salt Lake day, designed like they matter. Carried, worn, used. Three pieces, one color story (the lake from orbit: Salt Pink north arm, Sky south arm, a cream causeway between), one mascot (the brine shrimp), one label system (lab-style, filled in by hand). Nothing here is a souvenir; each is something a local touches between the airport and the lift line.

Working within the IN set only: 21-nasa-iss-great-salt-lake, 27-le-labo-mazarine-label, 31-spiral-jetty-uibel-aerial, 32-great-salt-lake-salt-crystals-imaggeo, 36-ald-leather-keychain-pouch, 47-district-vision-annapurna-eyewear-pouch, 50-brine-shrimp-dwr, 54-a24-primetime-vinyl, 23-loewe-craft-prize-2025-winner-aoki, 03-a24-houseplant-ashtray-matchbook, 06-parksproject-brass-french-press, 39-satisfy-merino-tube-socks, 40-ace-hotel-nalgene, 41-jmm-artemis-lighter-case. Lighters, ashtrays, candles, petit h, scraper and chairlift are not drawn from.

Fonts in the SVGs: Inter stands in for Saans, Georgia/Ivar Display for Ivar, Space Mono for the lab label. Swap to the licensed families in production.

## 01. North Arm / South Arm
File: `01-north-arm-south-arm-case.svg`

**IN references:** 47-district-vision-annapurna-eyewear-pouch (technical-fabric eyewear case, zip and woven label as the only detail), 21-nasa-iss-great-salt-lake (the two-tone lake, the straight causeway dividing it), 27-le-labo-mazarine-label (lab label with blanks filled at the counter), 41-jmm-artemis-lighter-case (a small carried case treated as the hero).

**Idea:** A goggle-and-glasses box case colorblocked exactly like the Great Salt Lake from the ISS: Salt Pink north arm, Sky south arm, and a 1/4 in cream binding tape running corner to corner as the Union Pacific causeway. The label is a Le Labo-style spec sheet with three blanks, Canyon / Date / For, so the shop fills in "Little Cottonwood, Jan 14" with a pen at the register and the case becomes a record of one day.

**Why this object and fabric:** It lives in the glovebox and the lift-line pocket, and locals carry sunglasses and goggles in the same car all winter, usually loose. 210D PU-coated ripstop over an 8 mm EVA core is the District Vision move, light and crushproof, and the ripstop grid takes flat color without sheen. A flat divider keeps goggles and glasses from scratching each other, with a lens cloth sewn to it.

**Technique and why:** Cut-and-sew colorblock, not print, so the split is structural and the causeway is a real binding tape, bartacked at both ends and continued around the gusset. Woven label with a pen-friendly cream face. One heat-transfer eyebrow on the back (coordinates and elevation, straight from the brand guide's eyebrow system), in cream on Sky.

**What a local would say:** "That's the lake from the plane. The pink half is the north arm, the railroad splits it." Then: "Canyon and date? Mine says Brighton, every day."

**Risk:** The pink/teal split needs to read as the lake, not as a generic colorblock. The label copy ("north arm pink, south arm blue, one causeway between") and the aerial on the hang tag carry that story; without them it is a nice pouch. Also the label blanks only work if retail actually fills them in; train the counter.

## 02. Field Recordings, Great Salt Lake
File: `02-field-recordings-lp.svg`

**IN references:** 54-a24-primetime-vinyl (a brand with a record label, sleeve as object, field-recording LP), 31-spiral-jetty-uibel-aerial (the spiral as the label shape, the jetty's arm running off the edge), 21-nasa-iss-great-salt-lake (split-color pour), 23-loewe-craft-prize-2025-winner-aoki (credit the maker: the recordist is named on every pressing), 03-a24-houseplant-ashtray-matchbook (small, bundled paper goods, the postcard download card).

**Idea:** A 12-inch LP of ten real Salt Lake sounds, nothing composed: wind on the north arm, the Wildcat lift's sheave wheels, snow on a windshield on I-80, a cold start and a scraper in Sugar House, TRAX door chimes, avalanche control in Little Cottonwood, brine flies lifting off Antelope Island, wind through the Saltair pavilion, gulls and a train on the causeway, salt crust underfoot at the jetty. The label is the Spiral Jetty, printed counter-clockwise as built, and the disc is poured in two colors at the causeway angle.

**Why this object and format:** The record is the one you put on when the snow is coming down. Vinyl makes a sound archive into a carried, shelved, gifted object, and the ASMR-leaning listener (the CD's "ASMR vinyl") is a real audience. 180 g, reverse-board jacket, uncoated label: everything matte and tactile.

**Technique and why:** Split-color pour (pink over blue) so every disc is a slightly different lake, like the real one. Two-color litho on the jacket, Salt Pink flood with an Ink spiral, the jetty's arm running off the top edge the way it does in the aerial. One-color Ink label; Side B is the same spiral at 60 percent, the jetty at low water. The recordist's name is set on the back in the credits line, on every pressing, not a liner note.

**What a local would say:** "Track A2 is the Wildcat lift. I'd know that hum anywhere." And "B5 is what the jetty sounds like when the water's down and you can walk out to it."

**Risk:** Rights and taste. The Spiral Jetty is a Holt/Smithson Foundation work; using its outline as a label needs a conversation with the Foundation, and the credit line on the back is there for that reason. Field recordings on resort property (Alta) and transit property (UTA) need permission to record and release. If the Foundation says no, the label becomes a plain salt-crystal outline and the piece survives.

## 03. Artemia
File: `03-artemia-pouch.svg`

**IN references:** 36-ald-leather-keychain-pouch (keys and a transit card, carried daily, one small mark on good leather), 50-brine-shrimp-dwr (Artemia franciscana, Utah's state crustacean, the mascot nobody has used), 06-parksproject-brass-french-press (daily-use object in honest brass), 23-loewe-craft-prize-2025-winner-aoki (the maker is the point: a local leatherworker, credited inside).

**Idea:** A 4.5 × 3 in zip pouch in full-grain veg-tan leather with an exterior card slot, so the transit card taps through the leather without opening it, and a brass swivel hook for keys. The only mark is a single-line brine shrimp, blind debossed on the front: eleven pairs of leaf legs, stalked eyes, brood sac, forked tail, drawn from the DWR photo. No logo, no words outside.

**Why this object and material:** Keys and the transit card are the two things every Salt Lake day starts with, and nobody has made them a nice object here. Veg-tan in Sego Lily (the palette's natural-leather color) starts pale and goes honey in a season, so the pouch records its owner's year. Unlined, 3.5 oz, painted edges: it should feel made, not finished.

**Technique and why:** Blind deboss with a brass die, 0.4 mm, no foil and no ink, because a shrimp in gold foil becomes a joke and a shrimp pressed into leather becomes a crest. The line weight is single and even so the die cuts clean; an illustrator redraws the SVG for the die. Inside, a 6 pt blind stamp with the care instruction ("Full grain. Made in Utah. Carry it daily.") and the maker's name.

**What a local would say:** "Is that a brine shrimp? Sea monkeys. That's the state crustacean, right?" Then it becomes the thing they explain to visitors, which is the whole job.

**Risk:** The shrimp has to be drawn with real confidence or it reads as a bug. The die art should go to an illustrator who draws natural history, not a logo designer, and it should be checked at the 2.75 in size on an actual deboss sample before the die is cut. Secondary risk: an exterior card slot adds a seam on the back; sample it so the card does not bow the leather.
