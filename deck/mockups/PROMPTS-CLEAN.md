# Merch prompts, plain language (no parameters, codes or reference tags)

Midjourney makes the design with your lane moodboard. Gemini puts it on the product. Attach the Midjourney result to Gemini first, then any shot reference photo.

## It Came From the Great Salt Lake
Midjourney: Vintage screen print t-shirt graphic, a 1950s drive-in creature feature poster crossed with a xeroxed 1980s punk show flyer. A gigantic brine shrimp with a segmented translucent body, feathery leaf-like legs, two eyes on stalks and a forked tail rears out of a pink salt lake. Low desert mountains behind. A crowd of tiny punks below with X's on the backs of their raised hands. Loose, gnarly brush and ink, heavy halftone, photocopier grain. Two inks only, black and faded salmon pink, on plain off-white paper. Flat artwork, no garment, no lettering.
Gemini 1: Print this graphic as a two-color water-based screen print, slightly cracked and sun-faded, across the front of a heavyweight boxy cream t-shirt. Add the title IT CAME FROM THE GREAT SALT LAKE across the top in drippy 1950s B-movie hand lettering in the same two inks, spelled exactly. Flat lay on pale concrete in soft window light, like a premium vintage band tee product page. No other text, no logos.
Gemini 2: Now show the same shirt worn by a skinny twenty-something with a buzzcut, standing against a sticker-covered door outside a small music venue at night, harsh on-camera flash, candid. The print matches the flat lay exactly. Mountains faintly visible down the street. No readable words anywhere else.
