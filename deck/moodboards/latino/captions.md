# Latino Salt Lake moodboard

## Lane thesis

This lane is not "Salt Lake does Hispanic Heritage Month." It is Latin life as it is already lived in the West: a lowrider parked under the Wasatch, a quinceañera posing in the Capitol rotunda, Chapulín cosplay at a Mexico match at Rice-Eccles, a jaripeo at the Fairpark during Days of '47. The words on the shirts come from the community, not from a tourism office, so no invented taglines. We borrow the merch logic that already works: Born x Raised gothic script and jersey block type, Kids of Immigrants plainspoken slogans, Paisa Boys fútbol crests, Bad Bunny's residency that turned a coquí into a tee. Paired with rótulo sign painting and lotería color, that is the type system. Photography should follow Estevan Oriol: honest, high contrast, in the neighborhood. The line has to be designed by Latino designers from Salt Lake County, co-signed by West Valley, Rose Park and Glendale, with a revenue share going to a community org like Centro Civico Mexicano or Comunidades Unidas. If the community would not wear it to a Sunday car show, we cut it.
