# Performance lane: "We run on Salt Lake"

## Lane thesis

Salt Lake is not a ski town with a city attached. It is a training ground with a zip code. People here get up at 5 to run the Bonneville Shoreline before work, skin up Alta in the dark, climb granite in Little Cottonwood on a lunch break, and ride the canyon road until their legs quit. They race the Wasatch 100 and the Speedgoat because the mountain is right there and so is the clock. This line should feel like race kit, not resort merch. Think bibs pinned to a singlet, laser perforation where you sweat, bonded hems, a reflective hit that only shows up in headlights, split times printed like a hard copy line from a 1990 Nike ad. Honest fabrics shown close up: Alpha Direct grid, mesh, ripstop. Photography is cold light, breath, dirt, effort. Nothing cute. The payoff line is simple: Salt Lake is performance. We just make the gear that proves it.
