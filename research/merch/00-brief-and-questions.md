# Visit Salt Lake merch deck: brief and open questions

Started 2026-10-26. Owner: Yid. Builder: Claude.

## The ask (as given)

A roughly 60-slide deck of fashion merchandise ideas for Visit Salt Lake (VSL). Not logo-on-a-tee. The brand ethos carried on the material. Every choice (garment, cut, fabric, trim, hat style, technique) has a stated "why" from the design and fashion world. Forward-looking for the VSL brand, but adhering to the current brand (link coming from Yid).

The deck has four parts:
1. Context: what modern merch is, who does it well, and the philosophy behind it (luxury, streetwear, performance, tech, Olympic, destination).
2. Foundation: materials, styles, techniques, the SLC symbol bank, the audience.
3. Executions: the capsule(s) with visualized pieces.
4. Build plan: manufacturer conversation, logistics, costs, timeline, and why people would buy it.

## Questions for Yid

### Brand and constraints
1. Link the current VSL brand (guidelines, logo files, type, color, photography style). Is there anything in the brand that is sacred, and anything you are itching to push past?
2. Is this an internal pitch to VSL leadership, or something you want to walk into a merch partner or manufacturer with? Who is the first audience of the deck, and what decision do you want them to make?
3. Does VSL sell merch today? If yes, what, where, and how does it do? If no, has anyone tried and why did it stall?
4. Who at VSL owns retail, partnerships, and legal? Any known rules around using "Salt Lake," the state "Utah" brand, Ski Utah, or 2034 Olympic language?

### Positioning
5. Pick a primary lane for the capsule, or rank these: (a) streetwear with SLC ethos (your brine-shrimp-on-a-jacket idea), (b) performance and ski-town technical, (c) elevated souvenir and objects (A24 and Hermès petit h logic), (d) all three as tiers.
6. Who is the first buyer: locals who want to rep the city, visitors who want proof of trip, the design and ski crowd nationally, or convention attendees? Rank them.
7. Price comfort: are we a $38 tee and $120 hoodie brand, a $65 tee and $220 fleece brand, or do we want a low tier and a hero tier?
8. How much weight on the 2034 Winter Olympics? A direct lean (even without using Olympic marks), a wink, or none yet?

### Taste and references
9. Name 5 brands whose merch you personally would buy and 3 you think are played out. I will treat those as the taste calibration.
10. Any SLC symbols you want in or out? For example: the Great Salt Lake and brine shrimp, the pink north arm, Spiral Jetty, salt flats, lake-effect snow, inversion, the grid and meridian, seagull, beehive, copper, the sego lily flag, 801, fry sauce, Saltair, the Jazz mountain colorway.
11. Tone: serious and quiet (Arc'teryx, Satisfy) or playful and weird (Brain Dead, Online Ceramics), or a mix by piece?

### Production and format
12. Realistic first run: 50 units of a few pieces for a test drop, or a 10-piece capsule at 200 to 500 each?
13. Do you want the deck to include real manufacturer names and quotes, or keep it at the "how to start the conversation" level?
14. Deck format: HTML deck, PDF, Keynote, Google Slides, or Figma Slides? Any VSL deck template to match?
