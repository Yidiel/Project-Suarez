## The show: three content pillars

Start at **1 post a week**, which matches your weekly review, and move to 2 a week in month 2. Every post is a story ("I did X", a real breakdown), not a tip. Package each one first: hook or title plus cover image, then make it.

| Pillar | Funnel | Format (repeatable) | Example episodes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Project Breakdowns** | Middle and bottom: trust, leads | Carousel plus a 45 to 60s voiceover video. Always the same 5 beats: brief → references → system → applications → result. | "We branded a Utah experience company in 41 days" (Discera). "A 40-page brochure cut to 20" (Constructora JQ). |
| **2. Place Studies** | Top: reach, recognition | Your photography in your house grade, then a 3-frame "what makes this place's identity work / what I'd change." Same walk-and-shoot setting each time. | SLC signage and type in the wild. Miami hotel identities. "If I rebranded [local place]" concepts. Pick these from the Top 30 list so the content doubles as outreach. |
| **3. Studio Ops with AI** | Top and middle: affinity with founders and creatives | Screen recording plus a talking head: "how a one-person studio runs like a team." | The proposal deck that builds itself. Brief to deck in a day. The agent roster. |

Score each pillar monthly on posts, saves, shares, follows, views and views month over month. Grade A to F. Give each one 4 tries before judging. Fix hooks before killing a pillar. Add the scorecard as a "Content" tab on the UFO Suarez dashboard.

**DECIDE:** archetype. I recommend Expert-as-story, with your Artist eye as the visual signature.
